Privacy Policy — Effective 8 May 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data FORGE Tactical Fitness ("the app", "we") collects, why, and how it is handled.

1. Data We Collect

  • Location data — GPS coordinates collected during ruck sessions while the app is in use or in the background. Used solely to map your route and calculate distance and elevation.
  • Account data — Email address used to create and authenticate your account via Supabase.
  • Training data — Sessions, routes, readiness logs, and nutrition notes you record. Stored locally on your device and, when signed in, synced to your personal Supabase database.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your training history, analytics, and squad readiness inside the app.
  • To provide AI coaching recommendations based on your logged sessions (processed via the Anthropic API; only aggregated stats are sent, never raw location data).
  • To sync your data across devices when you are signed in.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data is processed by the following sub-processors solely to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (supabase.com)
  • Anthropic — AI coaching recommendations (anthropic.com)

4. Data Storage & Security

Training data is stored locally on your device using encrypted on-device storage. When you are signed in, data is synced to Supabase servers protected by row-level security policies — only your account can read your data.

5. Background Location

The app requests background location access exclusively to continue recording GPS tracks when your screen is locked during a ruck session. Location recording stops as soon as you end the session. Location data is never transmitted to external servers.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by deleting your account from the Profile screen inside the app. This permanently removes all synced data from our servers. Locally cached data is removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Children

FORGE is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and notify you via in-app notice.
