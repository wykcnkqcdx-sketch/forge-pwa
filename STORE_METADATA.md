**Description** (4000 chars max)
```
FORGE is the training companion built for soldiers, operators, and anyone who trains like one.

Track every ruck, run, lift, and PT session with military-grade precision. FORGE combines GPS ruck tracking, squad readiness monitoring, nutrition logging, and AI coaching into one dark, no-nonsense app.

FEATURES

// GPS RUCK TRACKER
Log every ruck with live GPS tracking — distance, elevation, pace, and a calculated ruck score that accounts for weight carried, terrain, and body weight. Background tracking keeps recording even when your screen is locked.

// AI PROGRAMME BUILDER
Generate personalised training programmes based on your goal (strength, endurance, fat loss, or combat readiness), available days, session length, and current readiness level. Powered by AI, built around military fitness principles.

// CLAUDE COACHING
Get a daily coaching brief analysing your recent training load, identifying gaps, and giving you a single action to act on today.

// SQUAD READINESS
Log and track team readiness scores over time. Monitor who's green, amber, or red so you can plan training around real-world availability.

// SESSION LOGGING
Record PT, runs, strength, and ruck sessions with notes, terrain, weather, and weight data. Edit and review your full training history.

// ANALYTICS
Visualise training volume, session frequency, and readiness trends over 7, 30, and 90-day windows.

// OFFLINE FIRST
All data lives on your device. Cloud sync via Supabase keeps everything backed up when you're connected — but the app works fully offline.

FORGE doesn't hold your hand. It tracks your work.
```
